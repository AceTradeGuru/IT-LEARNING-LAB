Subject access and erasure

Every contact page carries an Export (GDPR Art. 15 / CCPA §1798.110) and, for owners, an Erase (Art. 17). The export includes the AI-generated material — the research brief written about them, the call plan built to persuade them, the analysis of what they said. Somebody asking what a system holds about them is entitled to the parts that were inferred, not just the parts that were typed in. Those are usually the parts they did not know existed.

Erasure suppresses their numbers before deleting them, so the next enrichment run cannot re-import the person who just asked to be forgotten.

What is recorded about every AI output

Every generated brief, plan, email and call analysis is written to the activity log with the engine that produced it, who triggered it, and when. When a model writes the words a salesperson says to a customer, somebody eventually asks where a particular claim came from.
