Why this gets harder to beat every month

Authority status, insurance, and safety scores are commodities — anyone can buy that feed. The signal nobody can buy is what this MC number did to the last broker who trusted it. Every check run here leaves a trace: which contact details were used, by whom, and whether it ended badly.

A hijacked authority can forge a certificate of insurance. It cannot forge a boring, stable, two-year dispatch contact history across four brokerages. That history is the moat, and it is worth more the day after you join than the day you joined.
